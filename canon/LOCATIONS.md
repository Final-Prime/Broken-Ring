# Locations — adventure-runtime additions

This file only resolves facts that `gameplay-lab`'s `canon/LOCATIONS.md` and
`canon/GEOGRAPHY.md` explicitly leave open. It does not restate or override
that source. Only the facts below are new, decided here for this project's
own canon going forward.

## Gnomecity

Source status in `gameplay-lab`: Wonderhills is `SOURCE_CANON` as a region —
gnomish burrows and workshop clusters scattered across the foothills linking
the Northern Peaks and Elenvale — but `GEOGRAPHY.md` explicitly notes that
"most settlements... remain `OPEN` until deliberately designed," and no
gnomish faction or leadership appears among the Ring's five major powers in
`FACTIONS.md`.

Resolved here: Gnomecity is the largest single gnomish settlement in the
Wonderhills, standing apart from — and west of — the wider scatter of
smaller burrow-and-workshop clusters that make up the rest of the region.
Gnomes are natively decentralized and never named the place themselves;
"Gnomecity" is an outsiders' name, plain and functional the way outside
names for gnomish things tend to be, and it simply stuck. There is no
official capital and no single ruler here, consistent with `gameplay-lab`'s
silence on gnomish politics — Gnomecity is a capital only in practice, by
being the largest hub of the Ring's clockwork and Trythium-machine
production. The ground is worked half-open across a huge area, and in many
places gnomish gearworks turn hundreds of feet down — `gameplay-lab`'s
dammed streams, water wheels, and steam vents, scaled up to their largest
and most productive expression anywhere in the Wonderhills.
