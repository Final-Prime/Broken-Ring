# Peoples — adventure-runtime additions

This file only resolves facts that `gameplay-lab`'s `canon/PEOPLES.md` explicitly
leaves open. It does not restate or override that source: names, ability scores,
and racial traits below are inherited unchanged from it. Only homeland, history,
and population facts are new, decided here for this project's own canon going
forward.

## Glammerkin, the Shifting Folk

Source status in `gameplay-lab`: "Their total population and original homeland
remain uncertain."

Resolved here: their homeland is the small island pair in the far northeastern
sea, off Frosthold's waters — fitting company for a people gameplay-lab already
places at "the First Era's twilight," since Glammerkin oral history on this
island reaches back toward the Cataclysm itself (see `HISTORY_AND_COSMOLOGY.md`
in this repo). The Glammerkin were once more widely scattered and present
across the Ring, but sustained distrust pushed them back until most of the
people withdrew specifically to this island. Individuals still wander the
wider world, often drawn to bardic and other charismatic paths, but the
majority is believed to remain on the island. They live at peace, in a manner
recalling the Lorienvale elves' reclusiveness, though without the elves'
nature-reverence.

Mechanics unchanged from `gameplay-lab`: Medium; Charisma +2, Intelligence +1;
speed 30 ft.; Mutable Form, Low-Light Vision, Veiled Mind, and Ancient Echoes.

## Solari

`gameplay-lab` describes the Solari only as they are now — elusive natives of
Havenhold, discovered by dwarven miners "long ago" — without saying how they
came to be confined there.

Extended here: at the time of the Cataclysm, Solari still lived on the open
surface. Afterward, one group descended into the deep places that became
Havenhold — the ancestors of the Solari known today. The rest stayed above
and, in what may have been religious instinct as much as literal pursuit,
tried to follow the setting sun west; failing to catch it, they stopped, took
root, and over millennia their already plant-touched bodies (`gameplay-lab`'s
Photosynthetic Constitution) completed the change into fully rooted trees with
reddish foliage — a separate, later contributor to Lorienvale's crimson forest
alongside the blood-trees described in `HISTORY_AND_COSMOLOGY.md`.

Mechanics unchanged from `gameplay-lab`: Humanoid, Medium; Dexterity +2,
Constitution +1; speed 30 ft.; Low-Light Vision, 30-ft. Antennae Telepathy/
Chirpspeak, an Adaptive Appendage, Photosynthetic Constitution, and poison
resilience.
