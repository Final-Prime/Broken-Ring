# Trythium — adventure-runtime additions

This file only resolves facts that `gameplay-lab` leaves open about
Trythium's ultimate origin — across its player-facing `canon/TRYTHIUM.md`
and `canon/CANON.md`, and even its DM-only `dm/SECRETS.md`. It does not
restate or override that source's `SOURCE_CANON`. Worth flagging: `gameplay-lab`
already answers almost this exact question in `dm/SECRETS.md` ("The Heart"),
kept hidden from players on purpose — what follows matches that hidden truth
closely, arrived at independently before this file's author had read it. Only
the parts still left open there are new, decided here. Treat this file as
GM-facing spoiler material, exactly like its source.

## The Heart

Source status: player-facing `gameplay-lab` only offers this as `RUMOR`/
`SUPERPOSITION` — "a mythical heart or central source," "a slumbering
consciousness," Trythium veins as "the world's circulatory system." Its
DM-only secrets confirm the Heart is real and sentient, and that it's the
reason Trythium negates the Void's draining influence — but explicitly
leave open "how conscious, communicative, instinctive, reactive, or
vulnerable" it actually is.

Resolved here: the Heart is a single vast, beating organ of living Trythium
lying at the point where the Ring's entire vein network converges — every
worked vein in Frosthold, the Tharnoks, and everywhere else ultimately
traces back to it. It is instinctive rather than deliberate: over years and
decades it actively regrows and redistributes Trythium into worked-out
veins, gradually spreading through fresh bedrock across the Ring, typically
a few tens to a couple hundred metres down — the real, unrecognized reason
Frosthold's mines have never run dry after so many centuries. Its exact
location is unconfirmed even to scholars and Trythium-sensitive dwarves;
some doubt it exists at all rather than being folk explanation for the
ore's persistence. It does exist, somewhere in the deep rock around
Havenhold. This also resolves `gameplay-lab`'s open question of whether
Havenhold's Solari radiance is a "more fundamental relationship" rather
than plain ecological adaptation — it is: the New Light is fed by
Havenhold's proximity to the Heart, the closest any reachable place in the
Ring comes to it.
