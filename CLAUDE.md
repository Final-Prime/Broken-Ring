# Broken Ring (public repo)

Public-facing site and reviewed story content for Final Prime's Broken Ring.
Draft lore, DM-only secrets, and in-progress design work stay in the private
lab — see `PUBLIC_RELEASE_POLICY.md` before adding anything here.

## Writing prose for this story

Before drafting or editing any narrative prose (prelude panels, future
chapters), read `docs/STYLE_NOTES.md`. It's a living log of Daniel's actual
prose patterns, extracted from his own text as they're found — not generic
writing advice. Match those patterns by default instead of falling back on
generic prose instincts.

When one of his corrections reveals a real pattern (not a one-off fix), add
it to `docs/STYLE_NOTES.md` with a concrete example, the same way the
existing entries are written — name the move, show it in his text, note
where else it could apply.
