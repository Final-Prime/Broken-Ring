The war ended without asking Garret Stone to die for it. Three years at Forest's End, two more north of it, and now only one thing left in his head: home.

Every mile changes what grows beside it

One last gate before the forest gave up and the grass took over.

OLD GUARD "Heading for Suncross? Been hearing strange talk out that way lately. Traders' talk. Nothing solid."

GARRET "Traders always talk. Two days, and I'm home."

Nothing solid is easy to leave behind

Garret Stone had counted the miles. Fifty. Twenty. Five. Nothing prepared him for the fifty-first, where there was nothing at all, only red-gold sand as far as the eye reached.

The counting stops before the walking does

Garret (barely a whisper) "...Tam?"

The Hollow doesn't answer. But for one second its head tilts, exactly the way Tam Ostler's always did, every time someone shouted his name across a yard.

Some things the sand didn't take
